What "Analyse" does

Opening a folder only lists its MIDI files: quick, and you can already browse, open and listen to any of them. Analyse reads the files and adds their parts, fragments and Search scores as it goes. Previously analysed files without Search scores are read again once.

  • For each file: length, tempo, meter, key (estimated from the notes when the file does not say), title and author.
  • For each part (channel): instrument, role (drums, bass, chords, melody), notes and the loops it repeats.

What you get: the Parts tab can search inside the songs ("a bass with a 2-bar loop at 120 BPM"), and the Length, BPM, Title and Author columns are filled for every file. Musical searches such as "snare roll" or "redoble de caja" rank drum fragments by how many standard MIDI snare notes (38/40) occur close together. This is a useful first pass, not a guarantee: some MIDI files use non-standard drum mappings. Files opened individually may be analysed on demand.

How long: depends on the files and your computer. The window stays usable but slower meanwhile; the analysis waits while you listen or send to LUNA. Pause and Resume whenever you like: what is done is kept.

Your files are only read: nothing is changed, moved or sent anywhere. Everything stays on your computer.

LUNA for Windows clears add-ons' saved data when it restarts, so after restarting LUNA the folder is listed again and the analysis may need running again.
